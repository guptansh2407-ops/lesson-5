Why your body needs Exercise

Regular physical activity is essential for maintaining a healthy body and mind. It helps build strong bones and muscles, improves cardiovascular health, and boosts mood and energy levels.

Tips to stay Healthy

  • Eat a balanced diet with plenty of fruits and vegetables.
  • Get at least 60 minutes of physical activity every day.
  • Drink plenty of water and limit sugary drinks.
  • Get enough sleep each night to help your body recover and grow.
  • Wash your hands regularly to prevent the spread of germs.
